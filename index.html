Frequently asked questions

Is Zeitra free to use?

Yes. Every Zeitra tool is free, requires no account, and runs entirely in your browser.

Do you collect or upload my data?

No. All calculations happen locally on your device. Zeitra does not upload, store, or track your inputs. Some tool selections are saved only in your browser's localStorage for convenience.

Are the time conversions accurate with daylight saving time?

Zeitra uses your browser's built-in IANA timezone database, so daylight saving time transitions are applied automatically. Results are educational estimates and may be off by an hour at the exact moment of a DST switch.

Does Zeitra support Chinese?

Yes. Use the EN / 中文 switch in the header to toggle the entire interface between English and Simplified Chinese.

How many cities does the World Clock support?

The World Clock ships with roughly 50 major cities across every continent. You can add or remove cities and your selection is remembered in your browser.

Is Zeitra available as an app?

Zeitra is a website that works on desktop and mobile browsers. You can add it to your home screen for an app-like experience; no installation is required.
