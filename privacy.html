Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-16

1. What we collect

Nothing, by design. Zeitra is a set of calculators that run entirely in your web browser. The dates, times, and city selections you enter are processed on your device and are never uploaded to a server, stored in a database, or sent to us.

For your convenience, a few interface preferences — such as your chosen language, light/dark theme, and which cities you added to the World Clock or Meeting Planner — are saved in your browser's own localStorage. These never leave your device. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

2. Cookies and advertising

Zeitra displays Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This is standard AdSense behavior and is governed by Google's advertising policies.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Where required by the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive, a consent management platform (CMP) is shown before any non-essential cookies — including advertising cookies — are set. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time through that platform.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or YourAdChoices.

3. Amazon Associates disclosure

Zeitra is a participant in the Amazon Associates program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites. As an Amazon Associate, Zeitra earns from qualifying purchases. This does not affect the price you pay. Affiliate links are marked as sponsored and use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener".

4. Children's privacy

Zeitra is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all calculations happen locally in the browser, no child data is transmitted to us in any case. If you believe a child has provided us information through another channel, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your rights

Because Zeitra does not collect or store personal data on a server, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete about you. For any questions about this policy, you can reach the site operator through the contact details published on the site.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice.
