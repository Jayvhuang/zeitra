Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Zeitra uses the browser's built-in IANA timezone database, so DST transitions are applied automatically.

Can I convert any date, including the past?

Yes. Pick any date and time; the converter works for past, present, and future instants.

How accurate is it near a DST switch?

Results are educational estimates and may be off by an hour at the exact moment of a DST change.
