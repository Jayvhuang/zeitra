Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown run live?

Yes. Once started it ticks every second showing days, hours, minutes and seconds remaining.

What time zone is used?

Your device's local time zone, matching the datetime you pick.

Can I count down to a past date?

If the target is in the past, the timer shows that time has elapsed.
