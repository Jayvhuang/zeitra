Frequently asked questions

How many cities does the World Clock include?

Zeitra's World Clock ships with about 50 major cities across every continent and lets you add or remove any of them.

Does the World Clock update automatically?

Yes. Each clock updates every second using your device's clock and the browser's timezone database.

Is my city list saved?

Your selected cities are stored only in your browser's localStorage, so they reappear next visit. Nothing is uploaded.
