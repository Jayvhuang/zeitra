Frequently asked questions

Which units can I add or subtract?

Days, weeks, months or years. Choose add or subtract and the result updates instantly.

What if the result lands on a missing month day?

The browser's date engine clamps gracefully (e.g. adding a month to Jan 31 yields the end of February).

Is it useful for deadlines?

Yes. It is handy for contract dates, subscription renewals and project milestones.
