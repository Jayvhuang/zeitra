Frequently asked questions

What does the Age Calculator show?

Exact age in years, months and days, total days/weeks/hours/minutes, the weekday you were born, and days to your next birthday.

Can I calculate age as of a past date?

Yes. Use the optional 'As of' field to compute age on any date instead of today.

Is it accurate for leap years?

Yes. The calculation accounts for varying month lengths and leap years.
