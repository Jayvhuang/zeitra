Frequently asked questions

What units does Date Difference report?

It reports the gap in days, weeks, and a calendar breakdown of years and months.

Does order matter?

No. The difference is always shown as a positive span regardless of which date comes first.

Are leap years handled?

Yes. The calendar-year/month breakdown uses real month lengths including leap years.
